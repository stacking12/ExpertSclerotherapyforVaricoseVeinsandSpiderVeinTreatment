Frequently Asked Questions
Have questions about sclerotherapy? We've got answers.
What is sclerotherapy and how does it work?
Sclerotherapy is a minimally invasive medical procedure used to treat varicose veins and spider veins. A specially formulated sclerosant solution is injected directly into the affected vein using a fine needle. This solution irritates the vein lining, causing it to collapse and stick together. Over time, the treated vein is absorbed by your body and fades from view — leaving your skin looking clearer and smoother.
How many sclerotherapy sessions will I need?
The number of sessions varies depending on the size, number, and severity of your veins. Many patients see significant improvement after just one or two sessions. Spider veins often respond faster, while larger varicose veins may require additional treatments. During your initial consultation at our Sunny Isles Beach office, we'll assess your condition and provide a personalized treatment timeline.
Is sclerotherapy painful? What is the recovery like?
Most patients experience only mild discomfort — a slight stinging or cramping sensation during the injection. The procedure itself is quick, typically lasting 30–45 minutes. Recovery is minimal; you can walk and resume most normal activities the same day. We generally recommend avoiding strenuous exercise and prolonged sun exposure for a few days post-treatment. Compression stockings may be advised to optimize results.
Who is a good candidate for sclerotherapy?
Sclerotherapy is ideal for adults who have varicose veins or spider veins causing cosmetic concerns or mild physical discomfort such as aching or heaviness in the legs. It is not recommended for pregnant women or individuals with certain clotting disorders. A consultation with our specialists at FL Sclerotherapy Injection Procedure will determine whether this treatment is right for you based on your medical history and vein condition.
How long does it take to see results from sclerotherapy?
Spider veins typically begin to fade within 3 to 6 weeks following treatment. Larger varicose veins may take 3 to 4 months to show full improvement. Results are long-lasting, though new veins can develop over time due to factors like genetics or lifestyle. Periodic maintenance sessions can help keep your legs looking their best.
